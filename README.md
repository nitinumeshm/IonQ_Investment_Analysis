# Investment & Financial Analysis of IonQ

## Objective
Analyse IonQ’s financial statements, stock trends, and business strategy to evaluate its long-term investment potential.

## Scope
- Financial statement analysis
- Ratio analysis
- 200-day moving average analysis
- Stock trend evaluation
- Business strategy assessment

## Tools Used
- Excel
- Yahoo Finance
- SEC Filings
- Financial Ratio Analysis
- Gemini

## Key Findings
- Revenue grew 3x from 2024 to 2025
- Assets increased significantly due to acquisitions
- Equity dilution impacted investor sentiment
- Long-term growth potential remains strong despite current losses

## Investment Recommendation
High risk, high growth potential.
